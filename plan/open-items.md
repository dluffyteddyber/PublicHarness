First draft 5 Nov 2026. Discuss any of these in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/dluffyteddyber/PublicHarness/discussions).

## Decisions for the maintainers
1. **Which idea is it?** (a) Volunteers donate compute, researchers run jobs on it (current working reading, like Folding@home). (b) People connect AI agents to a shared pool and a coordinator hands them research tasks. (c) Both: (a) first, (b) later.
2. **Name:** keep "Public Harness" as the working name?
3. **Main purpose:** general open AI research, or a focus (e.g. AI safety evals, science)?
4. **Ambition:** a prototype to prove the idea, or aiming to run it as a public service (that needs hosting, money, moderation)?

## Decided
- 5 Nov 2026: public repo on GitHub, licensed Apache-2.0, open to contributors (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md)).
